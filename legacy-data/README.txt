╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  HOW TO USE                                                      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                  ║
║  1. Double-click index.html to open in your browser             ║
║     (Chrome or Edge recommended for best PDF support)           ║
║                                                                  ║
║  2. First time: Go to Settings tab → verify company details     ║
║     and upload your logo                                         ║
║                                                                  ║
║  3. New Invoice tab → fill Bill To → add line items → Save      ║
║                                                                  ║
║  4. Preview button shows a clean invoice modal with             ║
║     Print and Download PDF buttons                               ║
║                                                                  ║
║  5. History tab shows all invoices with dashboard               ║
║     — mark Paid/Unpaid per invoice                               ║
║     — Edit or delete any invoice                                 ║
║     — Download individual PDF or export all to Excel            ║
║                                                                  ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣

╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  DATA STORAGE                                                    ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                  ║
║  All app data (invoices, customers, settings, logo) is stored   ║
║  in your browser's localStorage — 100% offline, no internet.   ║
║                                                                  ║
║  BACKUP TIP: Regularly export Invoice Excel from History tab.   ║
║  This keeps a permanent record even if browser data is cleared. ║
║                                                                  ║
║  To move to another computer: export all Excel files, then      ║
║  re-enter settings and re-upload logo on the new machine.       ║
